How to play

  • One sheet per phone. Everyone opens this page on their own phone; only the player whose turn it is taps Roll.
  • On your turn the sheet only offers what your roll allows: the two white dice added together, in any row, and one white die added to one colour die, in that colour's row. Take the white sum first if you want both.
  • Between your turns — from the moment you tap Done until you tap Roll again — the other players are taking theirs. You may cross off each of their white sums, so every number that is still legal is tappable. Tap nothing if none of them suit you.
  • Drag a row sideways to move along that colour's scale. Each colour scrolls on its own, and the padlock stays pinned to the right.
  • Left to right only. Crossing a number skips everything to its left for good.
  • The last number in a row needs five crosses in that row already. Taking it locks the row and earns you the padlock as an extra cross.
  • When another player locks a row, tap that row's padlock to close it on your sheet too.
  • Done with nothing crossed costs 5. Four of those ends the game, and so does the second locked row.

Scoring

  • 1 cross scores 1, 2 score 3, 3 score 6, 4 score 10 — and so on up to 12 crosses for 78. The padlock counts as one more cross.
  • Each penalty takes 5 off the total.
