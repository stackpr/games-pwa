      How to play

      • Everyone answers the same question a few times over, one player at a time on this phone. All the answers go in the bowl.
      • You take turns. On your turn you draw slips and get the others to say what is on them before the clock runs out. Got it is a point; Pass puts the slip aside until your turn ends.
      • When the bowl is empty the round ends. The same slips go back in for the next round.

      The three rounds

      • Describe it — say anything except the words on the slip.
      • One word — a single word, said once.
      • Act it out — no words at all.

      Scoring

      • Teams — two to six of them, taking turns. The whole team keeps what its clue-giver earns.
      • Each player scores — the buttons are everyone else's names. Tap whoever got it: they take the point and so does the player giving the clues, so getting through to anybody counts. The seats are whoever filled the bowl.

      Everyone has heard every slip by round two, which is the joke: the same answers get harder to give and easier to guess.
