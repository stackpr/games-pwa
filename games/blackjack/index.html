You always sit in the middle. Changing the table reshuffles and starts the next hand fresh; the bankroll carries over.

House rules

  • Blackjack pays 3 to 2. A win pays even money, a push returns the bet.
  • The dealer stands on all 17s, soft ones included, and draws to 16.
  • Double on any two cards, and after a split. You get exactly one more card.
  • Split any pair, once per hand. Split aces get one card each. Twenty-one after a split is a plain 21, not a blackjack.
  • The dealer peeks on an ace or a ten, so a dealer blackjack ends the hand before you have put more money out.

Not offered

  • No insurance, no surrender, no resplitting past two hands.
  • The other players at the table are there for the cards they use up. They play a simple, fixed strategy and their money is not tracked.
