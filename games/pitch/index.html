What counts

    Scoring a hand

    • Lock the bidder and the bid first, then count each side's points at the end of the hand.
    • Every point is taken by somebody, so the hand does not score until the sides add up to the whole total.
    • Every side keeps the points it actually took.
    • The bidder is the exception: take at least the bid or lose it. Falling short scores −bid instead of what was taken.
    • The sheet marks the bidder's number so a set hand is readable at a glance later.
