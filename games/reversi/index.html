Rules

  • Place a disc so that it traps a line of your opponent's between the new disc and one of yours — along a row, a column or a diagonal. Every trapped disc flips.
  • A move is only legal if it flips at least one disc. The dots show where you may play.
  • No legal move means you pass, and the turn goes straight back. The game ends when neither side can move — usually when the board is full.
  • Most discs wins. An equal count is a draw.

This game is also sold under a brand name, which is a trademark. Reversi is the original name and the one used here.
