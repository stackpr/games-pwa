Rules

  • Land your last seed in your own store and you go again.
  • Land it in an empty pit on your side and you capture it along with everything in the pit facing it. A facing pit that is already empty captures nothing.
  • Otherwise the turn passes.
  • Land your last seed in an occupied pit and you scoop that pit up and keep sowing, from there.
  • The turn ends when the last seed lands in your store or in a pit that was empty.
  • No captures, and never an extra turn — one tap can still run a long way round the board.

Both rule sets

  • Six pits a side, four seeds in each. Tap one of your pits to lift its seeds and drop them one per pit, moving towards your own store and on round the board.
  • You drop a seed in your own store but skip your opponent's.
  • The game ends the moment either side's six pits are empty; the other player sweeps their remaining seeds into their own store.
  • Most seeds in your store wins. An equal split is a draw.

Changing the rule set starts a new game.
