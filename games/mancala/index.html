Rules

  • Six pits a side, four seeds in each. Tap one of your pits to lift its seeds and drop them one per pit, moving towards your own store and on round the board.
  • You drop a seed in your own store but skip your opponent's.
  • The game ends the moment either side's six pits are empty; the other player sweeps their remaining seeds into their own store. Most seeds wins, equal is a draw.

Where your last seed lands

In your own store
In an empty pit of yours
… and take another turn?
In a pit that already had seeds

A capture takes nothing if the facing pit is already empty, and neither half of the empty-pit rule applies on your opponent's side. Changing any of these starts a new game.
