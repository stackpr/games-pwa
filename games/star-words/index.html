    How to play

    • The drawer taps Show me the word once at the start of the round, keeps the screen private, and draws on paper or a whiteboard. After that each word arrives face up.
    • No letters, no numbers, no words, no talking, and no pointing at things in the room. Arrows and question marks are fine.
    • Scoring deals the next card in the same tap, so a turn is one tap per word. Skip passes for nothing. The card carries its category, which the drawer may say out loud — it narrows fifteen hundred words down to fifty.

    Scoring

    • Two teams — the teams alternate and the whole team keeps what its drawer earns.
    • Each player scores — the buttons are everyone else's names. Tap whoever got it and they take the point, as does the drawer, and the next word is already up.
