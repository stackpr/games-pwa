Rules

Nobody owns the bees. You are both taking from the same pool, and the comb shrinks as you go.

Win by taking

×3three light
×4four mid
×5five dark
2 eachtwo of every kind

Your turn

  • Jumping is compulsory. If any bee can jump an adjacent bee into the empty cell beyond it, you must — and you keep jumping with the same bee while it can.
  • Otherwise put a bee in a cell and take a cell away. Pick a bee from the pool, tap an empty cell, then tap a cell to remove. Only cells at the edge of the comb come off.
  • If no cell can be removed, the placement is the whole move.
  • Cut a piece of comb off and it leaves the board — you take any bees that were on it.
  • When the pool is empty you place from what you have taken, which is a real cost late on.
  • A player with no move at all loses.

The bees are told apart by how light or dark they are, not by hue, so the game reads the same to anyone.
