Four 1s is 2000, five is 3000, six is 4000 — adding a die never lowers a score.

Playing a turn

  • Every kept die must score. Keeping a 1 and a 3 is refused, because the 3 earns nothing.
  • Bust is a roll with nothing scoring in it. The whole turn score is lost.
  • Hot dice: set all six aside and you get all six back, keeping the turn score.
  • Stop banks the turn. First to 10,000 wins.
