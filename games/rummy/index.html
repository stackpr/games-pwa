Scoring

Rummy

A scoresheet, not a dealer. It does not know what was played — it keeps the patterns, the hands and the running score.

Scoring a round

Enter how many points each player still had in hand. Whoever went out is on 0, and that is how the sheet knows who won the round — so if the stock runs out and nobody goes out, give everybody their count and leave nobody on zero.
