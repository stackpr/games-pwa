How scoring works

  • Made the contract: 10 points per bid trick, plus 1 for each overtrick.
  • Set: −10 per bid trick, and the overtricks count for nothing.
  • Nil: ±100. Blind nil: ±200. Scored per player, on top of the team contract.
  • A nil bidder's tricks still count toward their partner's contract, so a failed nil can still fill a bid.
  • Only a nil needs its own trick count, so a team with no nil enters one combined number.

What this does not do

  • No bag penalty — overtricks accumulate as points and never cost you 100.
  • Bids are not forced to total anything, and tricks are not forced to total 13. Enter what happened.
