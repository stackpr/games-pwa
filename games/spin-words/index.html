How to play

  • One phone, passed round. Everyone sees the same board, so nothing is hidden — the pass screen is only there so the phone reaches the right hands before anyone taps.
  • Spin, then call a consonant. Every one of that letter in the puzzle pays what the reel landed on. Get one and you keep the phone; call a letter that is not there and it moves on.
  • Bankrupt takes everything you have won this puzzle — but not what you have already banked by solving. Lose a turn just passes the phone.
  • Buy a vowel costs $250 out of this puzzle's winnings, and pays nothing. You need the $250 first.
  • Solve whenever you like — but the moment you tap it you are committed. The keyboard fills in the blanks, you get ten seconds, and the last blank you fill ends it there and then. Right, and this puzzle's winnings are banked for good. Wrong, or out of time, and the phone moves on.
  • Most banked after the last puzzle wins.

Which vowel? It costs $250 and pays nothing.
