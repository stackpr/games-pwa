Somewhere Between

One player sees a hidden spot on a sliding scale and gives a one-word clue. Everyone else drags the marker to where they think it is.

    How to play

    • The scale runs between two opposites — Cold to Hot, Trash to Treasure. A hidden target sits somewhere along it.
    • The clue-giver taps Show me the target, keeps the screen private, and gives one clue: the word on the card, judged against the scale.
    • Everyone else argues, then drags the marker to where they think the target is and locks it in.
    • 4, 3 or 2 points depending on which band you land in, and nothing outside them. The bands are only revealed after the guess.

    Why there is no number

    • The marker has no percentage on it, on purpose. A number turns the argument into arithmetic — "sixty-two, not sixty-five" — when the interesting part is more that way, less that way.

    Scoring

    • Two teams — the teams alternate and the whole team keeps the round.
    • Clue-giver and guesser — each round pairs one of each and they both take the points.
