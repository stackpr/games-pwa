    How to play

    • One person holds the phone and describes the big word. Everyone else guesses.
    • The describer may not say any word on the red list, nor any part of the big word itself.
    • Got it scores a point and deals the next card. Skip passes for nothing. Foul takes a point back when a banned word slips out — that call belongs to the guessers.
    • No spelling it, no rhyming it, no gestures. Everything else is fair.

    Scoring

    • Two teams — the teams alternate and the whole team keeps what its describer earns.
    • Each player scores — nobody is on a team. One person describes and the buttons are everyone else's names: tap whoever got it and they take the point, as does the describer. The same tap deals the next card.
