The Faker

Everyone sees the same sixteen words. Everyone but one of you is told which one is the word. Say a single word about it — and work out who is guessing.

Players
Names
Second hidden player

Categories
Pass the phone to Everyone else, look away.

One word each

Go round the table and say one word about the word. Then argue, and point at whoever you think is faking it.

The word was

Caught the Faker? They get one guess at the word to steal it back.

How to play

  • Pass the phone round first. Each player takes it, taps to see their card, and hands it on. Everyone sees the same sixteen words; all but one are told which word is the word.
  • The Faker sees the sixteen and nothing else, and has to work out what everyone is talking about while sounding like they already know.
  • Then one word each, out loud, round the table. One word — not a sentence. Too obvious and the Faker gets it; too vague and you look like the Faker.
  • Argue, then point. Everyone points at once at who they think is faking.
  • If the Faker is caught, they get one guess at the word. Guess it and they win anyway.
  • With six or more an Understudy joins: they are not told the word either, but they do get five words about it. Harder to spot than the Faker, and they may not know which of them they are up against.
  • Nothing is scored here. Who won an argument is not something a phone can settle.
