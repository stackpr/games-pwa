How to play

  • Six tries at a hidden word of four, five or six letters. Green is right, amber is in the word but somewhere else, grey is not in it at all.
  • The clock starts on your first letter, not when the page loads — so reading this costs you nothing.
  • Every try you did not need takes ten seconds off. Solving in three leaves three spare, which is thirty seconds. Being right early beats being quick and wrong.
  • Each length keeps its own board. Four letters is a different race from six.
  • Guesses are checked against seventeen thousand words on the page. Anything rarer is looked up online, and the answer is remembered so it is instant next time.
  • The clock stops while a word is looked up, so a slow answer costs you nothing. If none comes back, the guess is cleared and no try is used — pick another word.
  • Word lookups use dictionaryapi.dev and FreeDictionaryAPI.com, which draws on Wiktionary.
