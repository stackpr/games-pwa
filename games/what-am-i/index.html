    How to play

    • The guesser holds the phone up, screen facing out, so everyone else can read it.
    • The category is disclosed. Somebody reads it aloud before the guessing starts, so the guesser knows they are looking for an animal, a job, a landform — just not which one.
    • The room gives clues. No saying the word, no spelling it, no rhyming it.
    • Got it scores a point and deals the next word. Pass moves on for nothing.

    Scoring

    • Two teams — the teams alternate and the whole team keeps what its guesser earns.
    • Each player scores — tap whoever gave the clue that landed. They take the point and so does the guesser.
