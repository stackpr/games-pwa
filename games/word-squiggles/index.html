    How to play

    • Every letter on the board belongs to one word. Nothing is filler, and no letter is used twice — when you have found them all, the board is empty of unclaimed letters.
    • The heading is the only clue. Every word fits that theme.
    • Drag across touching letters to trace a word. Squiggles bend in any direction, including diagonally, and can double back. Drag onto the letter before last to undo a step.
    • One word crosses the whole board, edge to opposite edge. It scores gold when you find it.
    • A hint marks where a word begins — which is the genuinely hard part — and never more than that. It costs time, and each one costs more than the last: 15 seconds, then 30, then 45.
    • The clock starts the moment the board appears — working out the theme is part of solving it. Best times are kept per board size: a 7×9 is not the same job as a 5×7.
    • The board is built when you open it, sized to whatever words it drew, so the same theme is a new puzzle.
