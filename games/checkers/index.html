Rules

  • Jumping is forced. If any capture is available, you must take one — only the pieces that can jump will pick up.
  • Chains continue. If the piece you just jumped with can jump again, it must, and the turn stays with you.
  • Men move and jump diagonally forward one square. Reaching the far row crowns them, and kings move and jump in all four directions.
  • Crowning ends the move, even if another jump looks available from the new square.
  • You lose when you have no pieces left, or no legal move.
